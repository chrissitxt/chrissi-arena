CHRISSI'S ARENA

chrissi's Arena is a minimalist, retro-styled auto-fire roguelite where movement is the only input that matters. Your weapon aims and fires by itself. Collect gold, buy upgrades between waves, survive as long as you can.

PHASE 2 OF 5
✓
Phase 1: Prototype
A single HTML file, built to prove the core idea worked: auto-fire, movement-only controls, a shop between waves. It did. One of a dozen vibecoded prototypes, but the one that stuck. Three and a half months in, before I rebuilt it properly. Still lives in the repo, outdated decisions, lots of AI generated code and all.
●
Phase 2: Foundation (current)
It started with tearing the whole thing down to the foundation and rebuilding properly: a single file becoming a real, organized codebase. Now it's everything that comes after laying that foundation: visuals, atmosphere, sound, scaling, figuring out which mechanics actually belong here. The early decisions that decide what kind of house this ends up being, before the content-heavy work of Phase 3 starts filling the rooms.
Phase 3: Content Expansion
More items, enemies, and bosses. Deeper variety in how a run can play out. Nothing planned in detail yet, Phase 2 comes first.
Phase 4: Systems and Depth
Meta-progression, additional modes, more build variety, maybe. All still just ideas floating around, not real plans. That comes after the foundation.
★
Phase 5: Release Prep (the goal)
Final polish. Main focus is controller support, mobile support, and autosaves so a crashed tab doesn't cost you a run in progress, alongside a number of other QoL and accessibility improvements I already have concrete plans for. This is the actual target I am working toward.
✨
Stretch Goal: Steam Release
Getting here means a third full restructure, this time off vanilla JS and onto a real engine. I have ideas, and some early tests already went better than expected, but committing to that is still a long way off, and depends heavily on the feedback this game gets along the way. Q1 2028 at the earliest. Not a promise, just a hope.
